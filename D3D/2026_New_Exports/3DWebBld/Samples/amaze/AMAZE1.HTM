AMAZE

An Example 3-D Game for a Business Web Site

Welcome to Amaze! This is an example of how 3-D models can spice up your business web site. The object of the game is for the player to negotiate three small mazes - learning about your product or service in the process. Each maze contains an exit leading to the next maze. The final maze leads to an HTML page where you can test the product knowledge gained by the player. If the player passes the test successfully, the player wins a prize - such as limited distribution freeware.
